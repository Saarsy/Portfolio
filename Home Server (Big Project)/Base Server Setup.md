# What I Learned
## Take Your Time
This is the single thing I messed up on the most. I rushed through things and didn't fine-tune stuff, so later I had to redo stuff, and it just got really messy with all the files everywhere with random software downloaded that I thought would solve problems. When something is the foundation of something else, you have to take extra time to make it stable and workable. 

## Understanding How It Works
A key part of building a Home server for me was deeply understanding the concepts instead of just copy/pasting terminal commands from Claude, even though sometimes you don't understand, trying your best to understand makes the biggest difference and can take you from why isn't Claude fixing it to this is why it works now.

## Try To Use Less AI
I know a ton of people say this already but going to Google and doing it the old fashioned way COMBINED with AI assistance is the best combination. Never trust AI fully without some verification from trusted sources. The best strategy that I found was to feed AI the sources where it tells how to set the server up, and ask it to elaborate in terms that I would understand. This made the time to understand less and also developed a basic to deep understanding on how the server operated.

# Results
