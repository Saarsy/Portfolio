# What It Is
This is a magnetic travel case that can hold a GoPro Hero 7 Black and its charger with batteries. It can also hold a Micro SD to SD card adapter. The main feature of this is the ability to power the charger with the lid closed. This provides seamless charging on the go and makes it so that everything you need stays in one place safely.
# Why I Made It
I made this because of a few distinct realizations. The first was that I didn’t have a case for my expansive GoPro! Even though the GoPro is really durable, a case will always help out. The other realization was that the case could also charge the batteries, as the form factor of the charger is small and simple!

![Poster](/GoPro%20Case/Images/Before.jpeg)
# Design Process
First, I made a list of what I wanted to accomplish with this design:
- Satisfying open and close
- Easy to use
- Perfect fit spot for camera
- Tight fit spot for charger
- Perfect fit spot for SD card adapter

To make the case satisfying to open and close, I opted for a magnetic lid, with a ridge that helps establish grip and the right direction to open. Using precise calipers, I included 0.25 mm tolerance gaps for perfect fit and 0.15 mm tolerance gaps for tight fits.
