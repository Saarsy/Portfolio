# What It Is
The shelf katana holder is a holder meant for your katana (or whatever crosses your mind) that goes into your shelf so you can display your katana aesthetically without taking up shelf space.
# Why I Made it
Find me the shelf, Katana holder, because I wanted a place to put my katana so that it would take no space and also look good. I use this type of design because it doesn't use any screws, and it stays in place. It also offers advantages as it can cover any size of katana.
