# What It Is
This is a large print I made to hold a big manual tofu press. It has mounting holes on the back to mount on the wall and a big top that allows you to put the tofu press without having to look at it, as the top guides the press into place.
# Why I Made it
I made this because this tofu holder I had was very big and had a weird shape, as it had 2 long screws sticking out, making it unstable and impossible to store efficiently without a custom print.
