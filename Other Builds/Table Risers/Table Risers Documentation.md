# What It Is
This is a TPU print that elevates the height of your table. It is convenient when something like your side table is too low for your preference. To use it, you just screw it on to the bottom of your table and that's it!
# Why I Made it
I made this because we had recently bought a side table for our living room, but it was too low, and whenever you tried to place something on it, you would always think about how it would be much better if it were at a higher height. Knowing I had TPU, I knew I could design a solution for this. I quickly modeled and printed some TPU risers and added some wood-looking tape to it so it matched our flooring. This little friend solves the problem instantly and wasn't even noticeable, as if it had come with the table.
