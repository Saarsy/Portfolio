# What It Is
This is a print that I made to have a place to put my remote shell and third remote. It simply mounts to my side table and provides perfect fit holes for the remotes.
# Why I Made it
I made this because before I made this, the wires were all over the ground of my floor, looking horrible and hideous. I knew I had to design a solution, one fitting my situation. I took a good look at the table and realized it had high potential for organizing wires. I could use the tiny holes in the metal sheet on the back side of my table to insert pegs that basically clamp the wire to the table. This was an amazing idea as it solved more than half of my wiring problems and made my setup look 10x cleaner.
