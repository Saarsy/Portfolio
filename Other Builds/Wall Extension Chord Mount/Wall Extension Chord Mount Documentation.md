# What It Is
This is a 2 piece print I made to mount an extension chord to the wall for a cleaner desk setup.
# Why I Made it
This was around 60 percent of how I managed to hide all the cables under my desk. I made this because I wanted to hide my extension cord from the floor, and the only spot I could think of was near the top of my desk, but since my desk is pretty thin, I couldn’t directly mount it to the table, so I resorted to mounting it to the wall. I designed 2 pieces to mount it: the first one screwed into the wall solidly and provided a clean point to attach the second piece, which had all the things needed to mount the extension cord to it. I did it this way so that if I ever needed to replace the extension cord with something else in the future, I could use the mounting plate already there.
