# What It Is
This headphone hanger is a 2-piece build which is meant to mount to the bottom of a shelf to hold your headphones. Its big hanger supports all types of headphones with a cool design.
# Why I Made it
I made this because it was taking an unspeakable amount of space on my desk, just lying there with the wire taking even more space on my desk or getting ruined on the floor. I thought of a traditional headphone holder, but that still takes desk space, and the wire is still a problem. So I thought outside the box and saw the shelf right above my desk; this sparked an idea: what if I just mount a holder to this shelf? So I utilized the IKEA shelf’s screws that were already on the shelf to mount my hanger.
